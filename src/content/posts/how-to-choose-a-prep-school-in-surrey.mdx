Surrey has one of the highest concentrations of independent preparatory schools
anywhere in the UK, which is good news for choice and can make the decision harder,
not easier. Here's a practical way to narrow the field.

## Start with the logistics

Before comparing ethos or exam results, work out what actually has to be true day to
day: how far you're realistically willing to drive or how good the school's minibus
routes are, whether you need wraparound care, and whether day, boarding, or a flexible
mix of the two suits your family.

## Look past the Common Entrance results

Most Surrey prep schools will show you strong Common Entrance and scholarship results
— it's table stakes in this part of the country. The more useful question is where
their leavers actually go, and how a school supports children who aren't aiming for
the most selective senior schools, not just the ones who are.

## Visit on a normal day, not just an open day

Open days are choreographed. Ask to visit during an ordinary week so you can see
lesson changeovers, lunch, and how staff talk to children who aren't performing for
visitors. It tells you far more about the day-to-day culture than a prospectus can.

## Ask about the transition to senior school

Because most Surrey prep schools feed into a wide range of senior schools rather than
one linked school, ask specifically how they support Common Entrance, scholarship
preparation, and the emotional side of leaving at 11 or 13 — this varies a lot between
schools even within the same county.

## Compare more than one school in the same way

Use the same shortlist of questions at every school you visit — pastoral care, class
sizes, sport and music provision, SEN support if relevant — so you're comparing like
with like rather than being swayed by whichever tour guide you liked best.
