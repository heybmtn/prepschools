Great Walstead School is a co-educational preparatory school set in the West Sussex
countryside near Lindfield. It takes pupils from nursery age through to 13, with day
places and flexible boarding options.

The school sits on a large rural site and offers a broad programme of sport, music
and the arts alongside academic preparation for senior school entry.
