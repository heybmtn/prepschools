Aysgarth School is an independent preparatory school set in the North Yorkshire
countryside near Bedale, in the Yorkshire Dales. It takes pupils from around age 3
through to 13, with both day and boarding options. The school became fully
co-educational from September 2024, having previously educated boys only.

The school's rural site includes extensive playing fields and grounds, and it
prepares pupils for Common Entrance and scholarship entry to a range of senior
schools.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
