Cranleigh Preparatory School is a co-educational day and boarding prep school in the
village of Cranleigh, Surrey. It shares its site and setting with Cranleigh School,
the senior school it feeds, though it also prepares pupils for entrance to other
senior schools.

The school takes pupils from age 7 to 13 and places a strong emphasis on outdoor
education alongside its academic and sporting programme.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
