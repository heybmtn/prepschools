Rydal Penrhos Preparatory School is the junior part of Rydal Penrhos School in Colwyn
Bay, North Wales. It is fully co-educational, taking pupils from around age 3 through
to 11, before most pupils progress into the senior school on the same site.

Day and boarding places are both available, and the school shares many of its
facilities with the wider Rydal Penrhos campus.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
