Sunningdale School is an independent preparatory school for boys aged 7 to 13, set in
the Berkshire countryside near Ascot. It offers both day and boarding places, with
boarding forming a central part of school life for older pupils.

The school prepares boys for Common Entrance and scholarship examinations to a range
of senior schools, alongside a broad programme of sport, music and outdoor pursuits.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
