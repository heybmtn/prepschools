Ashfold School is a co-educational preparatory school set on a rural site near
Aylesbury, Buckinghamshire. It takes pupils from age 3 through to 13, with day places
and boarding available for older year groups.

The school combines academic preparation for Common Entrance with a wide range of
sporting, musical and creative activities.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
