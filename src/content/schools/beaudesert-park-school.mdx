Beaudesert Park School is a co-educational preparatory school in the Gloucestershire
countryside, on a rural campus near Minchinhampton in the Cotswolds. It takes pupils
from age 3 through to 13, with both day and boarding places available.

The school is known for its extensive grounds and emphasis on outdoor activity
alongside academic preparation for Common Entrance and senior school entrance exams.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
