Dragon School is an independent co-educational preparatory school in Oxford, taking
pupils from around age 4 through to 13. Alongside day places, it offers boarding from
the age of 7, and prepares pupils for entrance to a wide range of UK senior schools via
Common Entrance and scholarship examinations.

The school sits on a large city-centre site with extensive playing fields, and is
known for a broad co-curricular programme spanning sport, music, drama and outdoor
activity alongside its academic curriculum.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
