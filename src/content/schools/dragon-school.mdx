Dragon School is an independent co-educational preparatory school in North Oxford,
taking pupils from around age 4 through to 13. Pre-Prep pupils (up to Year 3) are day
only; boarding is available from Year 4, around age 8. The school prepares pupils for
entrance to a wide range of UK senior schools via Common Entrance and scholarship
examinations.

The school sits on a large North Oxford site with extensive playing fields running
down to the River Cherwell, and is known for a broad co-curricular programme spanning
sport, music, drama and outdoor activity alongside its academic curriculum.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
