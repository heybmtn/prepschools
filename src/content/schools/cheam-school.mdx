Cheam School traces its history back to the seventeenth century, making it one of the
oldest preparatory schools in England. Today it is fully co-educational, taking
pupils from age 3 through to 13, with day and boarding options.

Set on a large rural estate near Headley, on the Hampshire/Berkshire border, the
school combines an academic preparation for Common Entrance and senior school entry
with extensive grounds for sport and outdoor learning.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
