Cargilfield School is an independent co-educational preparatory school on the western
edge of Edinburgh. Founded in the nineteenth century, it is one of Scotland's
longest-established prep schools, taking pupils from around age 3 through to 13.

The school offers both day and boarding places and prepares pupils for entrance to
senior schools across Scotland and further afield.

For current details on admissions, fees and open days, see the school's own website.
